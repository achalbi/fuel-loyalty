01

The shape of the work

Three structural facts frame everything below. The app is strong on setup and rewards configuration; the large gaps cluster in daily settlement, a priced product catalog, reporting, and CRM intelligence.

  • The app is rupees-centric; the sheet is litres-centric. A transaction stores fuel_amount in ₹, not litres, while the requirement's settlement and per-visit capture are built on meter readings → litres → price. Bridging these is the single biggest architectural decision.
  • There is no product pricing anywhere. Fuel types carry only a code and name — no MRP, selling price, batch, or stock, and no representation of lubricants/oils/AdBlue. This "Product Catalog" is the keystone dependency for settlement, reports, and litres capture.
  • The app already exceeds the sheet on shifts & attendance. Beyond the requested shift creation and assignment, it ships rotating cycles, swaps, and attendance runs with an audit trail. That subsystem is a strength, not a gap.
02

Locked decisions

Confirmed 2026-07-21. Every spec and the roadmap honor these.

Q1 · Units

Litres are the source of truth

Capture meter readings / litres and derive ₹ from the catalog selling price. Puts the Product Catalog and litres model at the root of the plan.

Q2 · Surfaces

Both surfaces, in lockstep

Every feature ships on the Rails PWA and the native Android app, backed by the same JSON API. Budget ~1.5–2× per-feature effort.

Q3 · Operator KYC

Profile fields only

Add Photo, Address, Aadhaar #, ID-card photo (ActiveStorage + PII handling). Keep password login — no OTP / SMS this phase.

Open item to confirm — customer taxonomy. Assumed: OTP = fleet/credit accounts billed by litres (not "one-time-password"), TT = tank-truck credit, Drive-in = walk-in cash, Credit = credit account. Features E4, D5 and F2 depend on this reading.

04

The four foundations

Four capabilities sit at the root of the dependency graph. Almost nothing in the later phases can be built correctly until these land — build them first, in Phase 1.

Foundation 01

Product Catalog + Stock

Priced products (fuel + lubes/AdBlue) with MRP, selling price, batch and stock. The selling price is the multiplier that turns litres into ₹.

Unlocks D2 D6 D10 E1 · feeds the litres model
Foundation 02

Litres / Readings model

Turns the ₹-only transaction into a physical record — litres against a nozzle reading, with ₹ derived from the catalog price.

Unlocks D1 D6 B2 E1
Foundation 03

Customer master + type

Driver / supervisor / owner triple + the OTP/TT/Drive-in/Credit taxonomy. The join key for capture, targeting, reports and churn.

Unlocks B2 E1 E4 F2 E5 E6
Foundation 04

Push-token → customer link

Replaces today's anonymous push tokens with a customer/user association so a message can address an individual.

Unlocks F3 F4

Biggest changes

Ranked by effort × downstream fan-out × migration risk — the items to staff most carefully and review most closely.

Litres / readings model
Rewrites the core transaction write path both front-ends depend on; needs a backfill + dual-write window. Get it wrong and every derived-₹ feature is wrong.
Foundation · L
Product Catalog + stock (A5)
The deepest root of the dependency graph — the selling price it holds is the multiplier for all derived money. Wide fan-out into settlement and reports.
Foundation · L
Final-settle calc (D6)
The aggregate tying readings + lubes + discounts minus tenders together; must reconcile against physical litres. D7 and D9 hang off it.
D6 · L
Per-visit capture (B2)
Replaces the staff transaction flow on both surfaces with the full CustomerDetailsEntry form; the join point between operations and CRM. Four blockers.
B2 · L
Reports (E1)
Broad read-model over litres + customer + visit history across four time grains and three grouping dimensions. Cheap to get roughly right, costly to reconcile to the rupee.
E1 · L
Campaigns (F1) / WhatsApp-SMS (F4)
F1 is a new rules-engine surface; F4 adds a brand-new external messaging integration with its own compliance surface.
F1 · F4 · L
Admin settlement view/edit (D9)
An editable admin console over settlement records with cross-pump aggregation; also closes G1 (per-pump visualize + edit).
